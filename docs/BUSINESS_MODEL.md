# 💰 Business Model

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | ₺999/mo | 1 Instagram account, 500 AI replies/mo |
| **Pro** | ₺1,999/mo | Instagram + WhatsApp + Facebook, unlimited replies |
| **Agency** | ₺4,999/mo | Up to 10 seller accounts, white-label option |

## Revenue Projections

| Subscribers | Monthly Revenue |
|---|---|
| 500 Pro users | ₺1,000,000/mo |
| 1,000 Pro users | ₺2,000,000/mo |
| 2,000 Pro users | ₺4,000,000/mo |

## Target Market

- **Primary:** Instagram boutique sellers (clothing, shoes, accessories, gifts)
- **Secondary:** Facebook shop owners
- **Tertiary:** WhatsApp-based small businesses
- **Enterprise:** Social media management agencies

## Go-to-Market Strategy

1. **TikTok Ads** — Target boutique sellers and resellers
2. **Instagram Ads** — Reach active sellers on the platform
3. **Demo / Free Trial** — Let sellers try AI replies for 14 days free
4. **Referral Program** — Sellers refer other sellers
5. **Content Marketing** — Show time saved, sales increased

## Competitive Advantage

- 🌍 Multi-language support (Turkish, English, Arabic...)
- 🔗 Deep stock/inventory integration
- 💬 All 3 Meta platforms in one dashboard
- 🤖 AI trained for e-commerce / boutique selling context
- 💰 Affordable pricing for small sellers

## Why This Works

- Millions of active boutique sellers on Instagram worldwide
- Answering hundreds of DMs daily is exhausting and time-consuming
- AI can reply 24/7, check stock, guide orders — seller sleeps, sales continue
- Low churn: once set up, sellers won't go back to manual replies
