## 1. Brand Story & Mission

### Origin
SMTX was born from a simple truth: boutique sellers on Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook spend hours every day answering the same questions — *"Is this in stock? What size is this? How much for delivery?"* — while missing new sales that arrive at 2 AM.

### Mission
To give every independent seller an AI assistant that never sleeps, never misses a message, and always knows what's in stock — so they can focus on their business, not their inbox.

### Vision
To be the world's most trusted AI messaging layer for social commerce, starting in Turkey, scaling to the UK, UAE, and every market where small sellers hustle on social media.

### Values
| Value | What it means for SMTX |
|---|---|
| **Trustworthy** | Sellers hand us the keys to their customer conversations — we earn that trust every day. |
| **Smart** | Our AI must feel intelligent, not robotic. Every reply should sound human. |
| **Energetic** | We move fast, ship features, and celebrate seller wins. |
| **Inclusive** | We build for the solo seller in Istanbul, not just enterprise. |

## 5. Logo Usage

### 5.1 Logo Variants

| File | Usage |
|---|---|
| `logo/logo.svg` | Default — use on white or neutral backgrounds |
| `logo/logo-dark.svg` | Dark backgrounds (dashboard, landing hero) |
| `logo/logo-light.svg` | Light backgrounds (marketing pages, emails) |
| `logo/logo-icon.svg` | App icon, favicon, avatar, small spaces |

### 5.2 Logo Anatomy
- **Icon:** Overlapping speech bubbles with brand gradient, plus three spark dots (green, yellow, pink) representing the three platform colours.
- **Wordmark:** "SMTX" in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (800 weight), gradient fill on default/light variants, white on dark variant.

### 5.3 Clear Space
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "S" in the wordmark on all sides.

### 5.4 Minimum Size
- Full logo (horizontal): minimum 120px wide / 36px tall
- Icon only: minimum 24×24px

### 5.5 Do's ✅
- Use the provided SVG files — do not recreate the logo
- Use the correct variant for the background (dark/light)
- Scale proportionally
- Use on brand-coloured gradient backgrounds

### 5.6 Don'ts ❌
- Do not rotate or distort the logo
- Do not recolour the logo (use only provided variants)
- Do not add drop shadows or effects beyond what's in the file
- Do not use the icon wordmark at sizes below minimum
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a protective container
- Do not stretch the wordmark text

## 7. Voice & Tone Guide

### 7.1 Brand Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What it sounds like | What it doesn't sound like |
|---|---|---|
| **Friendly** | "Your AI is ready to take over your inbox 💬" | "The system is initialised." |
| **Smart** | "We noticed 40% of your DMs are about stock — here's what we did." | "Error: undefined response." |
| **Energetic** | "Boom — your first auto-reply just saved you 2 hours." | "Processing completed successfully." |
| **Trustworthy** | "Your customer data is stored securely and never shared." | "We may use your data for other purposes." |
| **Direct** | "Upgrade to Pro — your DMs won't reply themselves." | "We humbly invite you to consider our premium offering." |

### 7.2 Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Warm, welcoming, simple | "Welcome to SMTX! Let's get your first AI reply live in 3 minutes." |
| **Dashboard** | Confident, data-driven | "24 messages replied • 3 sales assisted • ₺840 saved in staff time" |
| **Error messages** | Calm, helpful | "Something went wrong — but your messages are safe. Try again or contact support." |
| **Marketing copy** | Bold, energetic | "Your AI. Always Selling. While you sleep, eat, and live your life." |
| **Emails** | Personal, conversational | "Hey [Name], your AI just replied to 12 messages overnight. Here's a summary." |
| **Legal / Privacy** | Clear, transparent | "We store your message data for 30 days to power AI responses. You can delete it anytime." |

### 7.3 Tone Don'ts
- Never use passive voice for success messages ("a reply was sent" → "your AI replied")
- Never sound robotic or technical in customer-facing copy
- Never use jargon without explanation
- Never be overly formal — this is a product for scrappy hustlers, not enterprises

## 8. Platform-Specific Usage

### 8.1 Instagram Post Style
- **Background:** Brand gradient or dark `#0D0D1A` background
- **Headline:** Plus Jakarta Sans, ExtraBold, white, tight letter-spacing
- **Accent elements:** Chat bubble shapes in gradient colours
- **Logo:** Bottom-right, white variant
- **Sizes:** Square 1080×1080 for feed, 1080×1920 for Stories

### 8.2 WhatsApp (Business Messaging)
- **Tone:** Short, friendly, professional — not salesy
- **Max message length:** 160 characters for auto-replies
- **Emojis:** Use sparingly — 1–2 per message maximum
- **CTA style:** "Reply YES to confirm" / "Tap to see options"

### 8.3 Dashboard UI (Dark Mode)
- **Background:** `#0D0D1A` → `#16162A` surface
- **Primary actions:** Gradient buttons (`#833AB4` → `#E1306C`)
- **Sidebar:** Dark with gradient active states
- **Charts:** Use platform colours (purple for Instagram, blue for Facebook, green for WhatsApp)
- **Typography:** Inter for all UI, Plus Jakarta Sans for metric numbers

### 8.4 Landing Page / Marketing Site
- **Hero:** Dark gradient background with large headline
- **Feature sections:** Alternate dark/light backgrounds
- **CTA buttons:** Always use primary gradient
- **Cards:** Light mode: white with subtle shadow; dark mode: `#16162A`

## 9. Spacing & Sizing Rules

### 9.1 Spacing Scale
Based on a 4px base unit (from CSS tokens):

| Token | Value | Common usage |
|---|---|---|
| `--space-1` | 4px | Icon padding, tight gaps |
| `--space-2` | 8px | Small gaps between elements |
| `--space-4` | 16px | Default content padding |
| `--space-6` | 24px | Section padding (mobile) |
| `--space-8` | 32px | Section padding (desktop) |
| `--space-12` | 48px | Large section gaps |
| `--space-16` | 64px | Hero section vertical padding |

### 9.2 Border Radius

| Element | Radius |
|---|---|
| Small buttons, badges | `--radius-full` (pill) |
| Cards | `--radius-xl` (16px) |
| Input fields | `--radius-lg` (12px) |
| Tooltips | `--radius-md` (8px) |
| Chat bubbles | `--radius-2xl` (24px) |

### 9.3 Shadow Usage
- **Cards on light:** `--shadow-md`
- **Cards on dark:** None, use border `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- **Active/focused elements:** `--shadow-glow-primary`
- **CTAs on dark:** `--shadow-glow-primary`

## 10. Accessibility

- **Contrast ratios:** All text must meet WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text)
- **Gradient text:** Only use for decorative large headings — never for body copy or interactive elements
- **Icon accessibility:** All SVG icons must include `<title>` and `aria-label`
- **Focus states:** Always visible — use `--shadow-glow-primary` outline

### Contrast Reference

| Combination | Ratio | WCAG |
|---|---|---|
| White on `#0D0D1A` | 19.2:1 | ✅ AAA |
| White on `#833AB4` | 4.6:1 | ✅ AA |
| `#1A1A2E` on `#F8F9FF` | 17.8:1 | ✅ AAA |
| `#B0B0CC` on `#0D0D1A` | 6.2:1 | ✅ AA |
